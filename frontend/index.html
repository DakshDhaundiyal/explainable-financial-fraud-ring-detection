Ablation Study

15 Controlled Experiments

Controlled component ablations isolating graph attention, continuous time encoding, and neighborhood depth against control baseline (ROC-AUC 0.8953).

Remove history neighbours

PR-AUC 0.0150 → 0.0087 (−42.2%)

F1 score collapses to 0.0000. Without multi-hop graph topology, an account observed in isolation cannot reveal structured smurfing rings or funnel accounts.

Remove time encoding

F1 collapses to 0.0000

Without Bochner harmonic continuous time encoding, a 5-minute burst of smurfing transactions and a routine monthly bill look identical to the model.

Remove temporal history features

ROC-AUC 0.8953 → 0.8379 (−5.7 pts)

Stripping rolling temporal features (amount ratios, transfer frequencies, delta seconds) sharply degrades discrimination across complex flow topologies.

Neighbour budget K

K = 1 fails (F1 0) · K = 10 selected

K = 1 fails completely (F1 = 0); K = 5, 10, and 20 all perform well. We selected K = 10 as the optimal trade-off between attention context and inference latency.

* Ablations are 15 controlled single runs (control ROC-AUC 0.8953), holding other hyperparameters constant. Note: a few variations (e.g. no dropout) scored slightly higher on the test split due to regularization trade-offs.
